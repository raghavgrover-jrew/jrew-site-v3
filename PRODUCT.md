# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sourcing directors, commodity managers and supplier-quality engineers at Tier 1 OEMs (JCB, Caterpillar, Hyster-Yale, CLAAS, Manitou, Doosan Bobcat class) evaluating JREW Engineering as a China+1 precision-manufacturing source. They arrive mid-diligence from a colleague's forward, a trade-show follow-up or a search for "forklift mast manufacturer India". They are sceptical, time-poor, fluent in grades, standards and PPAP, and they read numbers before adjectives. Secondary users: existing customers checking a capability claim before an audit, and engineering candidates.

## Product Purpose

jrew.co.in is the supplier-qualification asset. In ninety seconds it must do what a plant walk does in a day: show that a 1973-founded, public-limited Indian manufacturer holds 0.05 mm machining, welds to EN ISO 3834-2, runs a 40,000-a-year sole-supplier axle line at zero PPM, and ships container-stuffed export consignments from its own floor. Success is an RFQ opened, a drawing package sent under NDA, or a plant visit booked. Every claim on the site traces to the printed 2026 Corporate Profile or another approved source.

## Brand Personality

Engineering-led. Direct. Quantified. The printed brochure's voice: short declarative headlines in two colours, an eyebrow above, a hairline below, numbers large and labels small. Marigold yellow is the brand's only accent and it marks what matters in a sentence, not decoration. Photography is the real shop floor in daylight, cut-outs on white, and people at visual-management boards. No hyperbole, no motivational language, no invented precision.

## Anti-references

- The v1 dark site: 15 to 25 percent opacity labels, every section on pure black, sector photos behind 90 percent overlays, mid-sentence dimmed headlines, a "Scroll" cue, placeholder award years.
- The v2 graphite-and-forge identity: custom cursor, grain film, WebGL hero, chamfered everything, SLTP codenames in public.
- Indian peer sites that open with investor results and a chairman portrait; corporate sites whose home page is a news feed; hero sliders; consent walls over the first fold.
- Generic AI landing-page grammar: eyebrow above every section, 01 / 02 / 03 numbering as scaffolding, gradient text, glassmorphism, three equal feature cards, logo carousels as personality, em-dashes in interface copy.
- Any customer, figure or product name not printed in the 2026 brochure or otherwise approved.

## Design Principles

1. **Read as the printed brochure, built for the web.** The two-tone headline, the eyebrow-and-rule grammar, the left bar, the alternating pale rows and the black proof panels are the identity. The web adds what print cannot: filters, tables, sticky navigation, forms.
2. **Numbers are the copy.** Tolerances, capacities, years and counts carry the argument and are set in tabular figures so a sourcing engineer can quote them.
3. **Content before choreography.** Every word and image exists in server-rendered HTML before any animation runs; motion sequences, quantifies or navigates, and never gates.
4. **Two clicks to any spec, certificate or RFQ.** The qualification path is the information architecture.
5. **Cleared or absent.** If a claim cannot be traced to the brochure extraction or an approved source it is not on the site; it goes on the list for Raghav.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at or above 4.5:1, large display type at or above 3:1, yellow never used as text on white below display size. Full keyboard paths through the RFQ steps, the attachment filter, the sticky sub-nav and the product dialog, with visible focus rings. Touch targets at or above 44 px. Every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion alternative (static or crossfade) and reduced motion disables smooth scroll. Alt text describes the part and its context in the brand voice. Verified down to 390 px.
